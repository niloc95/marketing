# WhatsApp launch outreach — WebScheduler Business Listings

Copy-paste messages for inviting South African businesses onto
**listing.webscheduler.co.za** during the launch phase.

Internal document. The marketing site build only publishes the pages in its
allowlist (`scripts/build-marketing-site.js`), so nothing in this folder reaches
the live site.

---

## How to use these

1. **Pick the variant that matches the business** — general, professional, trades,
   or home industry. A message that names their actual trade gets replies; a generic
   one gets ignored.
2. **Replace every `[bracket]`** before sending. `[area]` is their town or suburb,
   `[trade]` is what they actually do. Never send a message with a bracket still in it.
3. **Send the opener on its own.** Wait for a reply before sending the follow-up —
   two unanswered messages in a row reads as spam.
4. **Reply to their actual question**, then point at the link. The FAQ answers at the
   bottom are there to be pasted mid-conversation, not fired off in sequence.

> The badge belongs here rather than in the opener. The listing genuinely is free, so
> the opener is honest without it — but mentioning it the moment someone shows real
> interest means the checkout page is never a surprise.

## Sending notes

- **Identify yourself in the first line.** Every message above does. It is the single
  thing that separates outreach from spam, both to the recipient and to WhatsApp.
- **Small batches, sent by hand.** Bulk-blasting marketing messages from the normal
  WhatsApp Business app to people who never contacted you is the fastest way to get
  the number banned. A few dozen a day, manually, to businesses whose numbers are
  publicly advertised.
- **POPIA s69** covers direct marketing, and it applies to businesses too. Honour any
  "stop", "not interested", or "remove me" immediately and permanently — keep a list.
- **If this scales**, it needs the WhatsApp Business Platform with an approved
  marketing message template and recorded opt-in. The copy above will need reshaping
  to fit template rules at that point.
- **Track what works.** Note which variant each contact received and whether they
  replied. After the first hundred, the data beats the copywriting.

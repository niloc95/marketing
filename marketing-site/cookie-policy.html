Cookie policy

Last updated 7 August 2026

The short version

This site uses two browser storage entries to remember your own choices, and Google Analytics if — and only if — you accept it. Analytics is off until you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time. There are no advertising cookies here at all.

Strictly necessary

These are set whatever you choose, because they exist to honour choices you made. They carry no advertising identifier, they are never sent to us, and they are not shared with anyone.

NameTypePurposeLifetime
xs-themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Shared with listing.webscheduler.co.za so the choice carries across both sites.Until cleared
ws-consentLocal storageRemembers your answer to the cookie banner, so we stop asking.Until cleared

This is a static site with no sign-in, so there is no session cookie and no login token.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people find useful. It is loaded with Google Consent Mode v2 and every storage category defaults to denied, so no analytics cookie is written and no measurement data is stored until you press Accept.

NameSet byPurposeLifetime
_gaGoogleDistinguishes one visitor from another.2 years
_ga_<id>GoogleKeeps session state for the property.2 years

If you decline, these are never written. If you accept and later change your mind, the same applies from that moment on — though cookies already set stay in your browser until you clear them. Google's privacy policy governs what it does with data it receives.

Changing your choice

Press the button below, or "Cookie preferences" in the footer of any page.

You can also clear site data in your browser settings, which resets us to asking again.

Third parties

Apart from Google Analytics, nothing on this site is fetched from another company. Fonts, stylesheets, scripts and images are all served from our own domain, so simply reading these pages does not expose your IP address to a content delivery network or a font host.

More

For the full picture of what we collect and why, see our privacy policy. WebScheduler Local keeps its own cookie policy, because it loads map tiles that this site does not.
